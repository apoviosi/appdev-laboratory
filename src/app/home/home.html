Welcome to the Pokémon League

Explore the rich history and formidable Gym Leaders across the Kanto and Johto regions.

Kanto Region

Kanto is the eastern region that set the standard for Pokémon battling and League progression. Named after Japan's Kantō region, it is home to Professor Oak's research laboratory in Pallet Town and the prestigious Indigo Plateau. Kanto boasts eight foundational Gyms specializing in distinct types ranging from Brock's Rock-type fortress in Pewter City to Giovanni's Earth Gym in Viridian City.

Johto Region

Located directly west of Kanto, Johto is deeply steeped in tradition, ancient mythology, and historic architecture. Centuries-old towers like the Bell Tower and Burned Tower in Ecruteak City celebrate legendary guardians, while traditional craftsmanship thrives in towns like Azalea. Its Gym Leaders uphold disciplined traditions across eight districts, culminating in the battle against the dragon master Clair in Blackthorn City.
